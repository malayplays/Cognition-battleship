Pixel Combat at Sea

A Pixel Art Battleship Game

How to Play

1

Place your fleet on the grid. Click cells to position ships. Use Rotate to switch orientation.

2

Once all ships are placed, press Start Game to begin the battle!

3

Take turns firing at the enemy grid. Red = Hit, Gray = Miss.

4

Sink all enemy ships to win! The AI will fire back, so protect your fleet.
